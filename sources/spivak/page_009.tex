\section*{}
\begin{center}
PREFACE
\end{center}

\noindent
I hold every man a debtor\\
to his profession,\\
from the which as men of course\\
doe seeke to receive countenance and profit,\\
so ought they of duty to endeavour\\
themselves by way of amends,\\
to be a help and\\
ornament thereunto.

\begin{flushright}
FRANCIS BACON
\end{flushright}
